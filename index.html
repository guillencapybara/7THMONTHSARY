A letter for you

Happy 7th monthsary, babyko ♡

Happy monthsary baby!! It’s our 7th monthsary, and yet we’re already getting close to our anniversary. I am very thankful gyud for you — nga ginasabot ko nimo permi, sa akong attitude and sa akong batasan nga usahay makapaapekto nimo, yet you still chose me to be with you for the rest of your life.

Even when naa tay mga lalis nga di ta magkasinabtanay, you still choose me. I love you for that gyud. Even during the times nga di ko kasabot sa akong sarili, you are there. Especially sa akong low days or down days, you’re still there — waiting, watching me grow, supporting me sa akong mga agenda and sa mga events nako. I love you so much gyud, babyyyyy.

Sometimes ma-hurt gyud ko nimo, especially when you’re angry and your words get too heavy. It does hurt, baby. But at the end, I know you love me, and I know sometimes it’s just anger speaking. And I’m sorry too — sa tanan nakong sala nga nabuhat sa imoha. Sorry gyud kaayo, baby. :(

Please be reminded that no matter what happens sa atoa, I’ll still choose you, over and over again. I can’t see myself without you, baby. And if you ever think nga I’m seeing another girl or burning out na ko sa atoa because of the things we go through — HELL NAHHHH BABEEE. That’s not true gyud.

I believe nga di gyud ta perfect. We will hurt each other sometimes, no matter how healthy we try to be, because we committed to each other and promised to grow together. We can expect the unexpected. We’ll make mistakes, we’ll learn, and we’ll keep growing.

And even when we hurt each other, wala gyud nato gibyaan ang each other. There was that time nga nagbulag ta, pero for me, it felt like a cooldown. Because after everything we’ve already gone through, di ko gusto nga usa ra ka problem ang mahimong ending sa tanan. Like… di siya maka-aesthetic, diba? RIGHT BABY? 😭

I’m thankful for us. I’m thankful for you. And I’m thankful to God nga gi-guide ta niya sa atong journey and wala ta niya gipasagdan along the way. We’re still here. Still choosing each other. And I’m so thankful nga gisabot ko nimo gyud. Fr ay, so thankful ko nga naa ka sa akong life, baby.

Once again, happy happy monthsary gyud sa atoa. I hope you do well sa imong acads, sa imong life, and please always be careful sa imong palibot. Don’t forget to put God first sa tanan nimong panghitabo.

I LOVE YOUUUU BABYKOOOO ♡
