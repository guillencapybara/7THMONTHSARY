7TH MONTHSARY WEBSITE
======================

Open index.html in a browser.

Everything is local:
- 15 original photos are in /assets/
- No external JS libraries are required.
- The "little song" is an original Web Audio melody generated by the browser.
- Click the envelope to trigger the flower/petal reveal and enter the site.

To replace the song with your own MP3:
1. Put your MP3 in the website folder, e.g. assets/oursong.mp3
2. In index.html, replace the Web Audio music button/script with an <audio controls> element,
   or tell ChatGPT to update the site after you provide the audio file.

To publish:
- Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, or any static hosting can host this folder as-is.
